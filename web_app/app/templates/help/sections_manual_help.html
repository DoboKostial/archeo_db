This form creates/updates section metadata and optionally builds a line between two measured points from tab_geopts.

  • Section ID: unique section/cut identifier (primary key).
  • Section type: standard, cumulative, synthetic (see Figs. 01 - 03)
  • FROM / TO point IDs: two different existing points defining the section endpoints.
  • Cut stratigraphy units:: all SUs that are cut by section.

Figures

Tips

  • Both endpoint fields may be left empty. Existing geometry and point bindings are then preserved.
  • The line uses exactly the two supplied points, in FROM-to-TO order, with their heights and the project EPSG.
  • Replacing manual/GIS geometry requires explicit confirmation. Failed builds do not save partial changes.
  • If geometry is not created, check that both points exist in tab_geopts and the project EPSG is configured.
